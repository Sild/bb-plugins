## What you get

Codex, Claude, and other installed providers appear as stacked sections in one Provider usage panel. Each section shows its plan, usage windows, percentages, and reset countdowns. Refresh updates every provider on the selected machine or shared account source.

## Requirements

Uses BB's built-in provider-usage plugin and its existing credentials, caching, and collectors. Providers without a usage integration cannot report limits. Providers reported as not installed are hidden; authentication and collection errors remain visible.

## Refreshing

The open panel refreshes every two minutes and when the window gains focus. Closing it cancels requests and stops its timers. A failed provider does not remove successful results from other providers.
